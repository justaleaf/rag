Neruv is a city-covered planet and the political heart of the galaxy. It was the seat of the Ordo Synth Spire and later the capital of the Dominion of the Void.

The skies are filled with air traffic, and the surface is layered with megacities.

Neruv housed the main shipyards where the Void Core was constructed in secret.

After the war, it became a symbol of renewal and democratic governance.
