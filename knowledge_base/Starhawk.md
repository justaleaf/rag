The Starhawk was a starfighter used by the Free Concord. Agile and armed with Pulse Cast cannons, it was designed for dogfights.

Jorin Solen flew a Starhawk during the Battle of Paxara and the assault on the Void Core.

The ships were produced on Lirea and could be launched from mobile bases.

Their signature was a high-pitched whine during dives.
