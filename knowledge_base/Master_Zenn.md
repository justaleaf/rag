Master Zenn was a revered elder of the Ordo Synth, belonging to the ancient Vulkar species. He served as a teacher and advisor for generations.

Despite his small stature, he was one of the most powerful beings in the Synth Flux. He trained both Torin Vex and Xarn Velgor before the fall.

Master Zenn foresaw the rise of the Void Pact and the tragedy of Xarn Velgor, but could not prevent it.

He lived on the swamp world of Dagobah and passed away peacefully, becoming one with the Synth Flux.
