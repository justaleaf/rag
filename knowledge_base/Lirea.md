Lirea is a lush, oceanic planet known for its beauty and strong connection to the Synth Flux. It was the home of Queen Sira Talen's mother.

The planet resisted the Dominion and was placed under blockade.

Lirean artisans crafted Resonance Shards used in Plasma Edges.

The Free Concord later established a training camp here for new Ordo Synth initiates.
