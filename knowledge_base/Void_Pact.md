The Void Pact was a secretive order that drew power from the Dark Surge of the Synth Flux. They believed strength comes through passion, control, and dominance.

Led by Lord Malvex, they operated from the Void Sanctum and sought to replace the Ordo Synth.

Their rituals involved meditation in darkness and trials of pain.

Though defeated, scattered cells of the Void Pact still exist in the outer systems.
