The Sky Runner was a YT-1300 light freighter famously piloted by Rex Iron-Cloak and Korvak. It was modified with illegal Rift Drives and heavy Pulse Cast turrets.

Known for its speed and durability, the Sky Runner played a crucial role in smuggling the plans of the Void Core from Neruv.

It survived multiple battles, including the assault on Paxara and the final conflict above Frostar.

The ship became a symbol of rebellion against the Dominion.
