Jorin Solen was a member of the Free Concord and a natural conduit for the Synth Flux. Born on Zarath, he was raised by his aunt and uncle, unaware of his lineage as the son of Xarn Velgor.

He was trained by Torin Vex and Master Zenn in the ways of the Ordo Synth. Jorin wielded a blue Plasma Edge and believed in peace and justice.

His journey was defined by conflict — with the Dominion of the Void, with his father, and within himself. He refused to kill Xarn Velgor when given the chance, showing compassion even in war.

After the destruction of the Void Core, Jorin helped rebuild the Ordo Synth Spire and later disappeared, leaving behind a legacy of hope.
