Synth Flux is a metaphysical energy field that binds the galaxy together. It is generated by all living things and can be harnessed by those trained in its use, such as the Ordo Synth or the Void Pact.

The Synth Flux has two aspects: the Light Flow and the Dark Surge. The Ordo Synth seek harmony, patience, and selflessness through the Light Flow. In contrast, the Void Pact draw power from passion, anger, and ambition via the Dark Surge.

Some beings are naturally strong in the Synth Flux, such as Master Zenn of the Vulkar species. Others, like Jorin Solen, show a rapid connection to it despite late training.

The Synth Flux can be used to enhance physical abilities, influence minds, foresee events, and even manipulate matter. Devices like the Holocron store ancient knowledge within the Synth Flux.

Though invisible, the Synth Flux is felt by sensitive individuals as a hum or resonance in the blood.
