The Ordo Synth was an ancient order dedicated to peace, knowledge, and the Light Flow of the Synth Flux. They served as guardians of balance in the galaxy.

Based in the Ordo Synth Spire on Neruv, they trained initiates from childhood and upheld strict codes of non-attachment.

The Ordo Synth were nearly destroyed by the Dominion of the Void during the Purge of the Spire.

Survivors went into hiding, waiting for a new generation to rise.
