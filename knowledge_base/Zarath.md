Zarath is a desert planet on the edge of known space, home to moisture farmers and smugglers. It has two suns and extreme temperatures.

Jorin Solen and Sira Talen were born here, though Sira was taken to Paxara as an infant.

The planet has ancient ruins suggesting early Synth Flux activity, possibly linked to the first Ordo Synth.

Xarn Velgor's suit was forged in the fires of this world after his defeat by Torin Vex.
