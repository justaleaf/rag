Sira Talen was a leader of the Free Concord and a skilled diplomat from Paxara. She was the twin sister of Jorin Solen, though they were separated at birth.

Sira played a key role in uncovering the plans for the Void Core and securing the plans from a stolen Sky Runner.

She later became a general in the resistance and fought in the Battle of Frostar. Though not a wielder of the Plasma Edge, she was strong in the Synth Flux and could sense disturbances in it.

Sira was known for her courage, sharp mind, and unwavering belief in freedom.
