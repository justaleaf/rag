Torin Vex was a master of the Ordo Synth and a guardian of peace during the rise of the Dominion of the Void. He once trained Xarn Velgor but was forced to duel him on Zarath, where he was defeated and believed to have perished.

In truth, he survived and lived in exile, guiding Jorin Solen from afar. He reappeared during the Battle of Neruv to deliver the plans of the Void Core.

Torin wielded a green Plasma Edge and believed in patience, wisdom, and the Light Flow of the Synth Flux.

He disappeared after the war, leaving behind journals stored in a Flux Vault.
