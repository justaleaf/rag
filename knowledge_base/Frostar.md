Frostar is an ice planet used as a hidden base by the Free Concord. Its harsh climate provided natural cover.

The Battle of Frostar was the first major conflict between the Free Concord and the Dominion, where Sira Talen led the defense.

The base was eventually discovered and attacked by Night Fang squadrons.

Jorin Solen was present during the evacuation and used the Synth Flux to collapse an ice tunnel, delaying the enemy.
