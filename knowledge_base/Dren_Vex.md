Dren Vex was the son of Torin Vex and a powerful user of the Dark Surge. Once known as Ben Solo, he fell to the Void Pact and became a feared enforcer.

He wielded a cracked Plasma Edge and sought to surpass his father.

In the final battle, he dueled Jorin Solen and was redeemed in his last moments, helping to destroy a Void Core prototype.

His legacy remains controversial.
