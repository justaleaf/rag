The Void Core was a massive battle station constructed by the Dominion of the Void under the direction of Lord Malvex. Designed to project unimaginable power, it housed a superweapon capable of obliterating entire planets.

Built in orbit around Neruv, the Void Core was powered by a controlled Synth Flux reactor. Its primary weapon could focus energy through a series of kyber-like crystals, allowing it to fire on targets across planetary distances.

The station was first used to destroy Paxara, a peaceful world and stronghold of the Free Concord. This act galvanized resistance across the galaxy.

Despite its defenses, the Void Core had a critical flaw: a narrow thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor. Jorin Solen exploited this weakness during the Battle of Paxara, firing a proton torpedo that triggered a chain reaction.

The destruction of the Void Core marked a turning point in the war against the Dominion.
