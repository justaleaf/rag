Xarn Velgor was a central figure in the Dominion of the Void, once a member of the Ordo Synth before his fall to the Void Pact. Born as Jorin Solen's father, he was believed to have perished in the fire of Mustafar, but was reborn in a mechanical suit that sustained his life.

Velgor wielded a Plasma Edge with unmatched skill and became the enforcer of Lord Malvex. He was driven by a desire to control the Synth Flux and eliminate all who opposed the Dominion.

Despite his dark path, a spark of Jorin Solen's father remained. In the final battle above Paxara, Velgor sacrificed himself to save his son from Lord Malvex, destroying the Void Core in the process.

Legacy: Xarn Velgor is remembered as both a tyrant and a tragic hero, a symbol of the struggle between control and redemption.
