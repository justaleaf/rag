The Plasma Edge is a weapon used by both the Ordo Synth and the Void Pact. It consists of a hilt that projects a blade of pure energy, powered by a Resonance Shard.

The color of the blade reflects the user's alignment: blue and green for the Light Flow, red for the Dark Surge.

Plasma Edges require skill and focus to wield. They can deflect Pulse Cast bolts and cut through most materials.

The design is ancient, with roots in the early days of the Synth Flux traditions.
