Korvak was a Vulkar warrior and loyal companion to Rex Iron-Cloak. He served as co-pilot and gunner aboard the Sky Runner.

Known for his growls and loyalty, Korvak fought in every major battle of the war.

He survived the destruction of the Void Core and later helped rebuild the Free Concord fleet.

Legends say he still patrols the outer systems, watching for signs of the Void Pact's return.
