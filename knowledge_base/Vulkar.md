Vulkar is a tall, hairy species from the planet Kashyyyk. They are known for their strength, loyalty, and deep connection to nature.

Korvak, co-pilot of the Sky Runner, was one of the most famous Vulkars.

The species played a key role in the ground battles of the war, using their strength and archery skills.

Vulkars believe in ancestral spirits, which they say are part of the Synth Flux.
