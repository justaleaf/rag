Lord Malvex was the ruler of the Dominion of the Void and master of the Dark Surge. He manipulated Xarn Velgor into serving him and sought to control the Synth Flux through fear and domination.

Malvex resided in the Void Sanctum on Neruv and wielded a red Plasma Edge with deadly precision.

He was ultimately defeated by Xarn Velgor, who turned against him to save Jorin Solen. His body was consumed in the collapse of the Void Core.

Legends say his voice still echoes in the Dark Surge, tempting those who seek power.
