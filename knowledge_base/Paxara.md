Paxara was a peaceful, green planet and the homeworld of Sira Talen. Known for its diplomacy and art, it was a member of the Galactic Senate.

The Dominion of the Void destroyed Paxara using the Void Core as a demonstration of power.

The event galvanized the galaxy against the Dominion and became a rallying cry: "Remember Paxara!"

No survivors were found.
